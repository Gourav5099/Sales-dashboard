# Tableau Visual Stories & Exploration

This directory contains Tableau packaged workbooks (`.twbx`) and visual storytelling exports.

### Planned Contents:
- **Workbooks**: Packaged Tableau workbooks connecting to the cleaned sales data.
- **Visual Stories**:
  - Regional & Geographic Sales Distribution (Maps & Symbol Maps)
  - Category Profitability & Discount Impact (Scatter Plots & Highlight Tables)
  - Customer Segment Breakdown (Treemaps & Bar Charts)
- **Exported Visuals**: PNG/PDF captures of worksheets and dashboards for portfolio display.
